# Saved cbr.ru responses

Responses read from cbr.ru on 2026-10-06, for the snapshot parsers'
tests (`scripts/cbr-snapshot.test.mjs`): the DailyInfo web service's
`KeyRateXML` (1 June to 6 October 2026) and `RuoniaXML` (20 September to
6 October 2026) responses, and the main part of the database pages of
the zero-coupon yield curve of federal loan bonds (5 September to 6
October 2026; calculated by the Moscow Exchange) and of inflation (2020
to 2026), with their scripts removed.

Source: Bank of Russia, https://www.cbr.ru/ (terms of use:
https://www.cbr.ru/user_agreement/).
